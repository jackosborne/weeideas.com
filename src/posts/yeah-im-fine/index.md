description: >-
  Sometimes it’s easier to tell someone you’re fine than to explain what’s actually going on. I wanted to explore that idea visually, taking a familiar phrase and repeating it until it becomes increasingly difficult to make sense of. As the words spiral inwards, the space between them disappears and the letters begin to overlap. The answer stays the same, even as everything underneath it becomes harder to untangle.
